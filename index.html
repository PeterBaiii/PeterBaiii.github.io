About Me

I am a master's student in Machine Learning at Carnegie Mellon University. I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering (Intelligent Software) from Nanjing University in 2026. Previously, I was a research intern at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Lingming Zhang.

My research interests lie in the theoretical and empirical foundations of modern machine learning, particularly how foundation models learn, adapt, and generalize. I am interested in how training objectives, optimization, and data shape representations and model behavior, and how different learning approaches—including pretraining, supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, and in-context learning—affect capabilities and generalization. I hope to study these questions through controlled experiments and mathematical analysis.
